How to Run the Mail Merge

Follow these steps after downloading your clean Excel list and Word template above.

1
Open the Word template
Download Parent ArmyDesign v2.docx from the templates section above. Open it in Microsoft Word.
2
Go to the Mailings tab
Click the Mailings tab in the Word ribbon at the top of the screen.
3
Select Recipients → Use an Existing List
Click Select Recipients → Use an Existing List… A file browser will open.
4
Select your clean Excel file
Navigate to wherever clean_leads.xlsx downloaded (usually your Downloads folder). Select it and click Open.
5
Select the "Leads" sheet
Word will ask which sheet to use. Select Leads$ and click OK.
6
Preview your letters (optional)
Click Preview Results in the Mailings ribbon to flip through and confirm names and emails are populating correctly.
7
Finish & Merge
Click Finish & Merge, then choose:
  • Edit Individual Documents — creates one big Word file with all letters. Good for reviewing before printing.
  • Print Documents — sends directly to your printer.
  • Send Email Messages — sends via Outlook. Set subject line, select Email_Address as the To field, click OK.
